# 60-Second Daily English Upgrade

**Date:** 2026-11-12

### Your Quick Boost for Natural English!

Ever want to kickstart a conversation or a project with energy? This week's phrase is your go-to!

#### Natural Phrase of the Week: "Get the ball rolling"

**Meaning:** To start something; to get an activity or process started.

**Example in action:**
"Let's **get the ball rolling** on this new marketing campaign by scheduling our first brainstorming session for Monday morning."

#### SpeakReady Micro-Tip:

**Use active voice for stronger communication!** Instead of "The report was written by Sarah," try "Sarah wrote the report." It's clearer, more direct, and makes your sentences more impactful.
