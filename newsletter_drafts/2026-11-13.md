# 60-Second Daily English Upgrade: Staying 'On the Same Page'!

Ever been in a team meeting or a group chat and felt like not everyone was fully aligned? That's where today's phrase comes in handy!

## Natural Phrase of the Week:
**On the same page**

This phrase means that everyone involved understands the situation, task, or information in the same way; there's a shared understanding.

## Example in a Sentence:
"Let's quickly go over the project details one more time to make sure everyone's **on the same page** before we start coding."

## Your Micro-Tip for the Day:
Use "on the same page" to confirm understanding and alignment, especially in group settings or when clarifying instructions. It's a professional and natural way to ensure clarity and prevent misunderstandings, much more engaging than simply asking, "Do you understand?"
