# 60-Second Daily English Upgrade

## Phrase of the Week: Get the hang of it

**Meaning:** To learn how to do something, especially something that is not obvious or easy at first.

**Example:** "Don't worry if using this new software feels awkward at first; you'll *get the hang of it* after a few tries."

## Micro-Tip: Active Application

When you learn a new phrase, try to use it naturally in your next conversation or create a few practice sentences. This active recall solidifies your understanding and helps you integrate new vocabulary much faster than just memorizing it from a list. The more you use it, the more natural it feels!
