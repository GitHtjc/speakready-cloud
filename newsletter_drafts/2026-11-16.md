## 60-Second Daily English Upgrade

**Natural Phrase:** To get the ball rolling

**Example:** "Let's **get the ball rolling** on this project meeting by discussing our objectives for Q4."

**Micro-Tip:** Use this idiom when you want to suggest starting an activity or discussion. It's an informal but universally understood way to initiate something. Instead of just saying "Let's start," using "Let's get the ball rolling" adds a touch more natural fluency to your English, making you sound more confident and native-like.
