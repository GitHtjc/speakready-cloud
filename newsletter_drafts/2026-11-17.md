# 60-Second Daily English Upgrade: Get the Ball Rolling!

Want to sound more natural and assertive in English? Today's upgrade helps you initiate conversations or tasks smoothly.

### Phrase of the Day: To get the ball rolling

**Meaning:** To start something; to begin a process or activity.

**Example:** "Alright team, let's *get the ball rolling* on our brainstorming session for the new marketing campaign. Who has the first idea?"

### Micro-Tip:

Instead of just saying "Let's start," use "to get the ball rolling" to imply an energetic and proactive beginning. It's perfect for meetings, projects, or even casual plans with friends. It suggests momentum and a shared effort to kick things off!
