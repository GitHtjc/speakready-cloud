# 60-Second Daily English Upgrade: November 19, 2026

## Natural Phrase of the Day: **"on the cusp of something"**

**What it means:** To be at the point when something important or exciting is about to begin or happen. It suggests being on the verge of a significant change or development.

**Example in context:** "With the rapid advancements in AI, it really feels like we're **on the cusp of something** revolutionary in technology, perhaps even altering how we learn and work forever."

**Micro-Tip for Fluency:** To sound more natural, try replacing generic phrases like "about to happen" or "almost ready" with more vivid expressions like "on the cusp of something." It adds a layer of anticipation and emphasizes the significance of the impending event.
