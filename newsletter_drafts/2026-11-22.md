### 🚀 60-Second Daily English Upgrade - November 22, 2026 🚀

**Today's Phrase:** "Get the ball rolling"

**What it means:** To start an activity or process; to make a beginning.

**Example in Action:** "Alright team, let's *get the ball rolling* on our new project by scheduling an initial kickoff meeting for Monday. We need to define our objectives clearly."

**Micro-Tip:** This idiom is often used in professional and casual settings alike. Think of it as a friendly but firm way to suggest initiation. Try using it when you want to propose starting something, whether it's a discussion, a task, or an entire project!
