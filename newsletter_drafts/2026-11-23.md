## 60-Second Daily English Upgrade: November 23, 2026

### Today's Natural Phrase: "The ripple effect"

**Meaning:** A situation in which one event or action causes a series of other events to happen, often in an unplanned or unexpected way, spreading out like ripples in water.

**Example in use:** "My decision to work from home full-time had a *ripple effect* on my daily routine, from waking up later to spending more time on hobbies and even reorganizing my living space."

### Micro-Tip:

Use "the ripple effect" to describe how one small action or change can have widespread, often unforeseen, consequences. It's excellent for explaining complex relationships in projects, social situations, or even personal habits. It adds a natural, visual layer to your explanations, making them more engaging and clear.
