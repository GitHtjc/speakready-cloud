# 🚀 60-Second Daily English Upgrade: Circle Back

**Natural Phrase:** To **circle back** (on something)

**What it means:** To return to a previous topic or discussion, or to revisit a task at a later time. It's a common and polite way to defer a conversation or task without dismissing it.

**Example in action:**
"Thanks for bringing that up, but I'm currently focused on the Q4 report. Let's **circle back** on the new project proposal first thing tomorrow morning."

**🎯 Micro-Tip:** Use "circle back" to manage expectations and demonstrate that you value the topic or task, even if you can't address it immediately. It's perfect for professional settings where you need to politely postpone discussions or follow-ups.
