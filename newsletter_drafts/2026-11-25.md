**Natural Phrase:** to grapple with something

**What it means:** To struggle to deal with or understand a difficult problem or issue.

**Example in action:** "Many societies are currently **grappling with** the rapid advancements of AI and their ethical implications, trying to find a balance between innovation and responsibility."

**Micro-tip:** This phrase is perfect for discussing complex, ongoing challenges in a sophisticated way. It shows you understand the difficulty involved. Try using it when talking about global issues, personal dilemmas, or intricate project problems! It's a great way to sound more fluent and thoughtful.
