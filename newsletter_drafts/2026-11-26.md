# 60-Second Daily English Upgrade

## Natural Phrase of the Week: "Just around the corner"

This phrase is perfect for talking about events, deadlines, or seasons that are approaching very soon. It adds a natural, informal touch to your speech.

**Example:** "The holidays are just around the corner, so I need to start my gift shopping soon!"

**Micro-Tip:** Use it to express anticipation or to gently remind someone about an upcoming event. For instance, "The project deadline is just around the corner, so let's finalize those reports!"
