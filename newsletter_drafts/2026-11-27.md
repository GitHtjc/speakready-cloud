# 60-Second Daily English Upgrade: Week of November 27th, 2026

## ✨ Natural Phrase of the Week: "It's a double-edged sword."

**Meaning:** This phrase means that a situation, choice, or decision has both positive and negative consequences. It implies something that appears beneficial also has a significant downside.

**Example:** "While working remotely offers incredible flexibility and autonomy, it's a **double-edged sword**; you gain freedom but might miss out on spontaneous team collaboration and networking opportunities." 

**💡 Micro-Tip:** Use this idiom to add nuance to your discussions, especially when you're weighing the pros and cons of a topic, describing a complex situation, or expressing a balanced perspective. It's particularly useful in professional settings or academic conversations where you need to show critical thinking beyond simple 'good' or 'bad' judgments.
