Chatting About Online Hobbies and Virtual Connections p51

Category

Everyday Small Talk

Level

Intermediate

Scenario

You're at a casual gathering, either online or in person, and someone asks what you've been doing for fun lately, especially related to online activities.

Textbook

"I like to play games online with my friends. It is fun."

Textbookcritique

While direct, this lacks detail and enthusiasm. It doesn't invite further conversation or elaborate on the 'why' behind the activity.

I've actually been getting into a lot of online board game nights recently – it's a fantastic way to stay connected with friends who live far away, and we've discovered some really cool new games that way.

Analyzing the Societal Impact of Emerging AI Technologies p53

Category

IELTS & TOEFL Speaking

Level

Advanced

Scenario

In a speaking test, you're asked to discuss the positive and negative influences of advanced technology, such as Artificial Intelligence, on modern society or education.

Textbook

"AI is good because it helps us learn and do things faster, but it can also be bad if we rely on it too much or it makes mistakes."

Textbookcritique

This response is overly simplistic and lacks the critical depth and sophisticated vocabulary expected at an advanced level. It doesn't offer nuanced perspectives.

Natural

While AI undoubtedly offers incredible potential for personalized learning and automating mundane tasks, we must also critically examine its ethical implications, particularly regarding data privacy and the risk of over-reliance on algorithms impacting critical thinking skills.

• risk of over-reliance on algorithms
• impacting critical thinking skills
