Category

Level

Advanced

Scenario

In an academic speaking test (e.g., IELTS Part 3 or TOEFL Independent Task), you are asked to discuss the potential positive and negative impacts of artificial intelligence on the job market and future employment opportunities.

Textbook

AI will change jobs. Some jobs will go, some new jobs will come. We must prepare for this.

Textbookcritique

This is a very simplistic and generic response. It lacks depth, academic vocabulary, and fails to elaborate on *how* AI will change jobs, *which* jobs might be affected, or *how* to prepare effectively. It doesn't demonstrate critical analysis.

Natural

Artificial intelligence is undoubtedly poised to profoundly reshape the global employment landscape. On the one hand, it's expected to automate repetitive tasks, potentially leading to job displacement in certain sectors. However, it's equally likely to spawn entirely new industries and roles, demanding a workforce with advanced digital literacy and critical thinking skills. The key will be proactive reskilling initiatives and robust educational reforms to bridge this evolving skills gap.

Natural Phrase of the Day: Get a handle on something

Meaning: To understand, manage, or gain control over a situation, task, or piece of information.

Example: "It took me a few weeks to *get a handle on* all the new software, but now I feel much more productive."

Micro-Tip: When you say "handle on," the 'le' in 'handle' often blends smoothly into 'on', almost sounding like 'hand-lon' /hændl̩ ɒn/. Practicing this flow makes your speech sound more natural!
