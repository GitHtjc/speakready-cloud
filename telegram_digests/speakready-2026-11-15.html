Hybrid work has problems. We need to talk more. Use video calls.

Textbookcritique

This response is too basic. It identifies problems vaguely and offers very general solutions without elaborating on *why* these solutions are effective or *how* to implement them. It lacks professional vocabulary and specific examples.

Natural

Navigating the complexities of a hybrid work model certainly presents its unique set of challenges, particularly around maintaining team cohesion and ensuring equitable access to information. To counteract this, fostering intentional communication channels, like dedicated virtual coffee breaks or leveraging collaborative platforms effectively, becomes paramount. We also need to standardize meeting protocols to ensure everyone, regardless of location, can contribute meaningfully.
